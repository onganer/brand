Reviewed 12 October 2026: https://step-to-health.lovable.app/#start

An educational walking resource supporting Artichoke Steps. Not a separate
software product, medical service, or replacement for the company website.

Journey: Genephera → Artichoke Steps → Walk Well walking guide → App Store.
Keep the main product information at /artichoke-steps and link both ways.
