## Artichoke Steps

### Header

**Artichoke Steps**

Understand your movement without turning every day into a target chase.

### Overview

Artichoke Steps is a private step-count companion from Genephera. It connects to
Apple Health and presents daily movement, recent patterns, and gentle personal
context in one focused view.

### Current release

**Version 2.2**

Version 2.2 adds a configurable daily step target, weekly step totals, and
best-day highlights, alongside refinements to the overall experience.

### Features

- Daily step count from Apple Health.
- Configurable daily step target.
- Seven-day step history.
- Weekly totals and best-day highlights.
- Weekly rhythm summaries and personalised insights.
- No account, advertising, or social leaderboard.

### Privacy

Apple Health access is read-only. Artichoke Steps does not collect Health data;
step information remains on the user's device. Permissions can be reviewed or
withdrawn in device settings.

### Availability

- Free on the Apple App Store.
- Requires iOS 17 or later.
- Designed for iPhone and iPad.
- Developer: Genephera.
- Seller: The Enginar Group Ltd.
